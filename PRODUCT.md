# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static multipage site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no build step, so it runs locally with any static server and publishes on any host. The client request area works without login: the visitor opens a ticket and receives a protocol, with the ticket kept in the browser.

## Users

Decision makers and office managers at small and mid-sized companies in Mogi das Cruzes and the surrounding region of São Paulo who need reliable IT support, plus employees who need to request help quickly. Their situation: an unstable network, a server that worries them, machines slowing down, or no internal IT team. The job: find a trustworthy local provider, understand what it offers, and start a conversation or open a support request without friction.

## Product Purpose

Present Protectlan Informática, its services and its credibility, and turn visits into conversations: WhatsApp, phone, email or a support ticket. Success means the visitor understands in seconds that Protectlan handles corporate IT, finds the relevant service, and makes contact with one obvious action.

## Positioning

A local, consultative IT partner for companies: it starts with a free assessment of the company's real needs, then delivers support with defined response times (24h SLA, 4h for urgent cases depending on location), from remote help to servers, structured cabling, security and cloud backup.

## Operating Context

Corporate IT support in Mogi das Cruzes/SP and region. Requests arrive by phone, WhatsApp, email or the client area; onsite visits are scheduled; remote support uses AnyDesk. Services are sold as monthly or premium plans after a free consultation. The public site is the first contact; the client area handles support requests.

## Product Principles

1. Contact first: every screen offers a clear path to WhatsApp or a support ticket.
2. Explain before selling: show what each service solves in plain language.
3. Local and human: the company is present in Mogi das Cruzes and answers by phone.
4. Proof through specifics: SLA, brands, service scope and real address instead of slogans.
5. Fast on any phone: the site must load and read well on mobile networks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard-operable navigation and forms, visible focus, labels on every field, and reduced-motion support.
